# 🇻🇳 ViLegalNLI: Vietnamese Legal Natural Language Inference Dataset

**📚 Course Project:** Natural Language Processing for Data Science

**ViLegalNLI** is the first NLI (Natural Language Inference) dataset developed specifically for the legal domain in Vietnamese. This project aims to serve the training and evaluation of deep learning models in the task of natural language inference on legal texts.

## 📚 Introduction

Even though Vietnamese is spoken by over 90 million users, it is still considered a low-resource language in NLP. Notably, specialized NLI data for the legal domain is particularly scarce. ViLegalNLI was built to bridge this gap, with the following goals:

- Create a high-quality dataset covering a wide range of legal topics
- Support the research and training of Vietnamese NLI models
- Promote the advancement of NLP for low-resource languages

## 🏗️ Dataset Details

- **Number of samples:** 9,667 premise-hypothesis pairs
- **Source:** 3,232 Vietnamese legal documents across 23 domains
- **Labels:** 
  - `0` (Entailment)
  - `1` (Neutral)
  - `2` (Contradiction)
- **Topic classification:** 26 topics such as "finance", "education", "real estate", "civil", etc.
